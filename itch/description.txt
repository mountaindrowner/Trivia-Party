TRIVIA HOLY PARTY

A loud, colorful Bible trivia party for kids, families and youth groups. Play solo on a phone, or put it on the TV and play together.

Answer 8 Bible questions. Miss one? Head to the Trial Room and win a minigame to keep your lamp lit. Then race Grumbles to the gate in the final round!

WHAT'S INSIDE
• 1,150 Bible questions, plus 180 final-race rounds
• 24 quick minigames: Find the Lost Sheep, Walls of Jericho, Noah's Pairs, David's Sling and more
• 3 levels for every age: Little Lambs (5–8), Mighty Warriors (9–12) and Legends (13+)
• Challenge Modes, with a secret mode to unlock
• Versus: 2–4 players take turns on one device
• Daily Challenge: 5 new questions and a minigame every day, with a streak to keep
• Holiday Rush: special Thanksgiving and Christmas minigames, in season only
• 6 animated Story Scenes to unlock, each in its own art style: David and Goliath, Jonah, Daniel, Noah, Elijah and the Empty Tomb
• 3 looks to choose from: Victory Lobby, Neon City and Holy Fire
• Your host, Barnabas the donkey, cheers you on (and teases you a little)

HOW TO PLAY
Tap or click the answers. On a keyboard, press 1–4 to answer. It plays in any modern browser, on phones, tablets, computers and TVs.

FOR PARENTS AND LEADERS
Questions are drawn straight from Scripture, written for a non-denominational Protestant audience, and checked for kid-friendly content. Bible battles and danger are told without anything graphic. Free to play, with no ads and no sign-up.
