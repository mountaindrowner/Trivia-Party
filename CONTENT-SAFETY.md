# Content Safety Review

Trivia Holy Party is played by children from about 5 to 14+ in homes and churches. This file records the review
of every piece of player-facing text: the content decisions, the theology and fact fixes, and the things a church may
want to know before using it.

## Decisions from the project owner

After the first review, the owner made these calls. The game follows them:

- **Bible stories are told as the Bible tells them.** Ehud, Samson's jawbone and lion, Elisha and the bears, the plague
  of blood, Lot's wife and martyrdom questions are all in the game. They aren't graphic, and they are part of the text.
- **Barnabas teases the player.** Lines like "Too slow! Even a tortoise would have tapped by now" and "Don't choke" are
  part of the host's personality. The DEFEATED and TOO SLOW stamps stay too.
- **No extra child-protection notes on lessons.** The lessons for "turn the other cheek," "love your enemies" and
  "keep forgiving" state what Jesus taught, with no added commentary.
- **Theology and accuracy fixes stay** (below), including the change to the secret-mode unlock text.
- **The game is non-denominational Protestant.** Content follows the Protestant canon and Protestant readings. It
  doesn't make room for Catholic or Orthodox positions (book counts, Mary, the saints). It still avoids taking sides
  on questions Protestant churches disagree about among themselves, such as baptism, communion and end-times timelines.
- **Wine is mentioned where the Bible mentions it** (Cana, wine as a wrong answer). Mild irreverent humor in wrong
  answers is fine, e.g. "cookie" next to the Last Supper bread, or Joseph and Mary as a wrong answer for the lying
  couple in Acts 5. Bible wording like "undergarment" stays.

## Theology (no side-taking between Protestant churches)

- **Baptism:** no longer says it only "pictures" salvation. It now follows Peter's own wording (1 Peter 3:21).
- **Isaiah 53:** the lesson quotes Isaiah and Peter instead of stating one theory of the atonement.
- **The secret-mode unlock** no longer suggests that winning games "opens the way to God." The way is open
  "because of Jesus, not because of anything we do" (Hebrews 10:19–20).
- **Lost Sheep lines:** the Shepherd comes looking for you, and answering only "gets you back in the game." Rescue is
  never something you earn.
- **Smaller fixes:**
  - "Jesus is the Lion King" became "Jesus is that King."
  - "Matthew wrote for Jewish readers," stated as fact, was softened.
  - "Malachi is the last book" now says "in Christian Bibles."
  - "Respected fathers didn't run" now says "in that culture."
  - Purim: "attack the Jews" became "harm the Jewish people. Esther's courage saved them."
  - Jacob's favoritism toward Rachel's sons is named as the cause of the brothers' jealousy.

## Wording kept from the first review

- **Gamer slang:**
  - "Bro," "sweaty" and "cracked" were swapped for words that work for every player.
  - "Legendary pull" became "Legendary answer."
  - "No mercy" became "No hints, no help."
- **Zacchaeus, Hannah, Nebuchadnezzar:**
  - Zacchaeus: "Who climbed a tree to see Jesus over the crowd?"
  - Hannah: "the priest misunderstood her."
  - Nebuchadnezzar: "When God humbled King Nebuchadnezzar."
- **Hosea:** the question about Hosea 11 (God teaching His child to walk) replaced the one about Gomer.
- **Other wording:**
  - "People with disabilities" replaced "the disabled."
  - "Fishers of People" replaced "Fishers of Men."
  - Name fields say "Nickname."

## Fact check

Every question, wrong answer, reference, lesson, final-round option, verse and minigame list was checked against the
Bible text (NIV/ESV) in two passes. The first pass was by the author. The second was by four independent reviewers, one
per section of the content. Each had only the text, with this rule: the right answer must be right, no wrong answer can
be arguably right, the reference must contain the fact, and lessons and quotes must be accurate.

**First pass:**
- **David** was the youngest of Jesse's eight sons. The game had said "of eight brothers," but he had seven.
- **Day Jesus rose:** a wrong answer said "the first day," which is actually right. It's now "the second day."
- **Creation week:** the seas are gathered on day 3.
- **Daniel** was taken to Babylon years before 586 BC.
- **The Septuagint** was begun about 250 years before Jesus.
- **The two good spies** were the only two *from their generation* to enter the land.
- **Moses** appeared with Jesus at the Transfiguration. The claim that he "stood in the land" was removed.
- **Esther** is now "Which of these books never mentions God?", because Song of Songs doesn't either.
- **Eutychus** fell asleep "while Paul preached late into the night."
- **Ehud's** lesson matches Judges 3:16: a short sword strapped to his right thigh, under his clothes.

**Second pass:**

## The content expansion (about 765 new questions, 120 final-race prompts)

The question bank was roughly tripled, from 385 to 1,150, plus 120 new final-race prompts. Every new item went through
the same standard as the originals, in four steps:

1. **Writing.** One writer per mode. Each read the existing questions, this file and the mode's description first.
2. **Removing repeats.** An automatic pass across all files, then a keyword search, dropped repeated facts. Writers on
   different levels had often picked the same story (Peter's coin in the fish appeared three times). The lowest-level
   copy was kept.
3. **Independent fact check.** One checker per mode, who hadn't written those questions, read every question, every
   wrong answer, every reference and every lesson against the text. Problems were fixed or the question was replaced.
4. **Topping up.** Each level was filled back to its target, and the duplicate check ran again.

**Examples of what the fact check caught:**
- A question said David found Saul in a cave. It was Saul who came into the cave where David was hiding.
- A question said Jehoiada hid young Joash. Jehosheba did.
- "Barnabas" was a wrong answer for who opposed Peter at Antioch, but Galatians 2:13 says Barnabas also drew back.
  Leah's "the LORD has seen my misery" sat next to Hagar's "the God who sees me".
- Lessons that went beyond the text:
  - The Bereans being "praised by Paul" (Luke says it, not Paul).
  - Peter "used the coin" (the text only records Jesus telling him to).
  - Jesus' family "wasn't wealthy".
  - "The bread and cup point to His body and blood". This takes a side on communion, so it's now just the quote.
- Misquotes:
  - Matthew credited with Zechariah's wording.
  - "That is Christ" for "who is Christ" (Galatians 3:16).
  - "Wash all of me!" for Peter's actual words (John 13:9).
- Translation-dependent answers. Bilhah and Zilpah as Jacob's "wives" became Leah and Rachel. NIV and ESV wordings
  are noted where they differ.
- The Gomer question came back in one draft and was removed again, per the decision above.

**The limit:** the checkers couldn't reach Bible Gateway from the build environment. The check is a careful expert
reading of the NIV/ESV text, not a line-by-line comparison with a live copy. Lessons that rely on history outside the
Bible are hedged with "about" or attributed to a source:
- Suetonius on Claudius.
- The Taylor Prism.
- Translation dates (Wycliffe, Tyndale, Erasmus, Luther).

## Second full safety review (all 1,150 questions and every line of text)

After the expansion, every player-facing line was re-read for safety and appropriateness by eight reviewers working in
parallel: one per mode, plus one for the final-race prompts, host lines, Story Scene script, Daily Challenge and
Holiday Rush text. Each worked from the owner's decisions above, so the cases already ruled fine were not re-flagged:
biblical violence told plainly, Barnabas's teasing, and no added child-protection notes.

**Result:**
- **Must fix:** nothing. A keyword sweep for sexual, gory, crude and insulting terms found nothing either.
- **Applied:** 45 smaller fixes.

**Fairness to Jewish people:**
- Good Samaritan: "the religious leaders kept their distance" became "a priest and a Levite walked past."
- The tenants parable: the lesson now names the listeners (Matthew 21:45) and widens the warning to everyone.
- Stephen: "the people's ancestors" became "the council's ancestors." The lesson notes Stephen was a Jewish believer
  speaking like the prophets.
- Two wrong answers that made Jewish observance look trivial (handwashing, Sabbath walking) were replaced.
- "So they will follow strict rules" is no longer a decoy for Jesus' "life to the full."

**Accuracy and hedging:**
- Disputed dates are hedged: Isaiah 53 "centuries before," Isaiah naming Cyrus, Psalm 22.
- Mark's authorship is "early church tradition."
- "A crowd cheering us on" became the actual words of Hebrews 12:1.

**Age:**
- A clue describing how King Zimri took his own life was replaced.
- A question that sent kids to the childbirth-purification law now asks about Mary and Joseph's offering in Luke 2.
- The "sons of David" prompt (seen from age 9) swaps Amnon for Adonijah.
- Thanksgiving's "Rolling your eyes at Mom" became "at a grown-up," so it doesn't assume a family.

**Trick wrong answers:**
- "Eating vegetables" for Daniel (he did, Daniel 1).
- "King" and "Teacher" for "I am the Good ___."
- Moses as "not Abraham's family" (he's a descendant).
- "The Samaritans" for "didn't believe in resurrection" (arguably true).

**Reviewer suggestions the owner turned down (reverted):** all made for Catholic and Orthodox readers, or as
reverence changes.
- Catholic and Orthodox book counts were taken out.
- The Dead Sea Scrolls question says "Old Testament" again.
- "His brothers" is a wrong answer at the cross again.
- Wine and "Juice" are back as wrong answers.
- "Cookie", "Joseph and Mary" and "undergarment" are back.

**Kept on purpose (owner's decisions):**
- Samson "tore apart a lion" (Lambs).
- Elisha "don't roast the prophet" (Legends).
- "Not even close, my guy." It's the one host line that uses a gendered address; swap it in `js/data.js` if you'd like.

## Privacy

- **No data leaves the device:** no accounts, chat, ads, links out or analytics.
- **Saved only in the browser's local storage on that device:**
  - the player's name, best score, XP, modes beaten and questions learned
  - Versus player names
  - the skin and mute settings
- **Name fields** now say "Nickname" (and "Player 1 nickname" in Versus), since the game is used on
  shared and church devices.
- **One outside request:** fonts load from Google Fonts, which sees the device's IP address. For a church or school
  deployment, self-host the font files so the game makes no outside requests at all.

## Motion, flashing and sound

- **Reduced motion:** everything respects the device's reduced-motion setting, which turns off animations, rain, embers,
  confetti and glitches.
- **Flicker:** flickering effects (the Trial Room sign, the hologram host, the logo glitch) flash fewer than 3 times per
  second, under the usual photosensitivity threshold. They should still be checked by someone familiar with
  photosensitive kids before wide use.
- **Sound:** the mute button silences everything, sound effects and music, and the setting is remembered. The 🎵 button
  (and the Jukebox) turns off just the music.

## Adding content later

- Every question needs a reference that contains the fact, and a lesson that is true to the text.
- Wrong answers must be clearly wrong, never "arguably right."
- Test any new mode at phone width before shipping.
