# SIGNAL DIVIDE — One-page Host Guide

## Before guests arrive

- [ ] Identify **two separate supervised zones** that are hard to overhear across.
- [ ] Check internet on the **host phone and two guest phones**. A QR works only when phones can reach the server.
- [ ] Keep your host phone charged/bright enough for scanning. Players can enter the join code manually.
- [ ] Confirm who is playing and whether you (host) are one of the 12–15.
- [ ] Tell everybody: "Stay in your zone. No texting or signals across zones. Keep your card secret unless you choose to show it. Move only when the app tells you."

## The 45-second teaching script

> "There are two secret teams: Signal and Static. Signal needs the Beacon and Jammer to finish **apart**. Static wants them to finish **together**. You get a private card and a zone on your phone — your zone is not your team. Talk with people in your own zone and decide who to trust. You'll choose a leader, and the leader decides who moves to the other zone at the end of each round. We play three rounds: three minutes, two minutes, one minute. You send two people in the first exchange and one in each of the next two. You can show someone just your faction or your full card, but nobody has to. **The role card never changes when you move zones.** At the very end we reveal who wins. If we have an odd number of players, there's also a neutral Forecaster who predicts the winner. Bluff about the game, not about anyone's real life."

## Actual game flow

| Phase | What you do |
|---|---|
| Lobby | Show QR / code, check names and ready count; lock roster at 12–15 |
| Deal | Instruct everyone to go to phone-assigned A/B and tap `I'm here` |
| Round 1 | Start 3-minute timer; leaders select 2 outgoing per zone |
| Exchange 1 | Wait for both locked selections; send 2 from A to B and 2 from B to A; check arrivals |
| Round 2 | Start 2-minute timer; leaders select 1 outgoing per zone |
| Exchange 2 | Wait for moves/arrivals |
| Round 3 | Start 1-minute timer; leaders select 1 outgoing per zone |
| Final | Wait for moves/arrivals; Forecaster predicts if present; app reveals faction winner |
| Repeat | Use `Rematch` to reshuffle roles and zones |

## Card controls

- **Peek at My Card:** press and hold; release hides it.
- **Show Faction:** big team label/icon only (e.g. Signal vs Static).
- **Show Full Card:** team, special role, objective.
- **Hide Now:** immediately cover.
- **Not in first version:** swapping actual role cards, passing phones, direct in-app chat across zones.

## 12–15-player quick counts

| Players | Zones | Teams | Neutral |
|---|---|---|---|
| 12 | 6/6 | 6/6 | None |
| 13 | 7/6 | 6/6 | Forecaster |
| 14 | 7/7 | 7/7 | None |
| 15 | 8/7 | 7/7 | Forecaster |

## Things most likely to go wrong

**Someone's phone doesn't scan QR:** type the short code instead; show higher brightness.  
**Someone taps the wrong zone:** pause, place them correctly, and use host correction if needed.  
**Leader refuses to pick:** a brief grace timer triggers server-selected legal transfers; pause if needed.  
**Someone has no cell/Wi-Fi:** help them reconnect before moving, or abort and use manual paper slips.  
**Players can overhear both rooms:** move the zones farther apart. The game gets less interesting if both sides hear all the secrets.  
**Someone leaves unexpectedly:** don't delete them mid-game. Pause, then restart or abort.

## Good group norms

- Keep physical zones within the event's supervision boundaries.
- No personal accusations. Claims about *game roles* are fair play; making fun of real people isn't.
- Let quieter players participate without pressuring them to show a card.
- Play a quick first round with the basic cards before adding any experimental powers.

## If there is no service

A paper fallback can mimic the core game: make one private role slip per person using the counts above; split into zones; select leaders verbally; use a phone stopwatch for 3/2/1; exchange 2/1/1 people; compare Beacon and Jammer at the end. This is **manual play, not connected multiplayer** and the host has to organize the deck and track the transfers. Prepare slips before the trip if reception is uncertain.
